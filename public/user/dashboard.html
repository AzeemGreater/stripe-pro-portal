🔑 Your License & Plugin Activation

Use your account email and activation key to activate the Shield Pro Stripe plugin on any WordPress website.

How to activate in WordPress:

  1. Go to your WordPress Admin > WooCommerce > Settings > Payments > Stripe.
  2. In the Activation Gate, paste your Email and License Key.
  3. Click Activate & Connect.
  4. Your website will instantly connect to this portal and show up in your sidebar!
